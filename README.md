- `contact.html` — quote/contact page

## Before publishing
Confirm these with Andy first:

1. Exact shop hours
2. Whether they truly offer 24/7 emergency/mobile service
3. License, bonded, insured, and certification wording
4. Real service pricing ranges
5. Real photos/videos from the shoot

## Positioning
Real local Portland locksmith shop for car keys, fob programming, rekeys, lockouts, residential locks, and commercial door hardware.

Do not use fake 24/7 claims, fake license claims, or template copy. Locksmith customers are already suspicious. Trust comes first.
