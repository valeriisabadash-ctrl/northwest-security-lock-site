Help for the complete lock, key and door problem

Northwest Security & Lock provides automotive locksmith service, residential locksmith service and commercial locksmith service. Work includes compatible car keys and programming, home rekeying and smart locks, business door hardware, master-key systems and access control.

A service request starts with the details

Tell the team what is happening, where service is needed and what kind of lock, door, vehicle or access system is involved. For a vehicle key, have the year, make and model ready and explain whether a working key remains. For property work, describe the existing hardware and the result you need.

The next step depends on compatibility, hardware condition and access authorization. Confirm service details, availability and pricing with the team before work begins.

Visit the Portland shop or discuss mobile coverage

The shop is located on SE Division Street. Call ahead for specialty keys or hardware questions. Mobile coverage includes Portland, Gresham, Happy Valley, Clackamas, Beaverton, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Milwaukie and nearby communities.
