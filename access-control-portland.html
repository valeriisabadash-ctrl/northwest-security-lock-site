Electronic access only works when the door and hardware work together.

A keypad or reader cannot compensate for a dragging door, failing closer, weak latch, poor alignment or incompatible locking hardware. Access-control planning starts with the full opening.

Can you add keypads to existing doors?

Often, but the door, lock preparation, latch, strike, closer and code requirements must be checked first.

Do you program cards and fobs?

Yes. Card and fob programming is available for supported systems and authorized users.

Can access control work with a panic bar?

Many systems can integrate with compatible exit hardware, but the opening and life-safety requirements must be reviewed.
