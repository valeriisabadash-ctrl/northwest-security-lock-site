Vehicle details matter before dispatch

Automotive locksmith work varies by vehicle. A basic metal key, transponder key, remote-head key, proximity fob, and push-to-start smart key are not the same job. The year, make, model, trim, key type, and current location help determine what tools and parts may be needed.

Before calling, check whether every key is lost, whether a key is locked inside, whether the ignition turns, and whether the vehicle battery has power. Accurate details prevent wasted trips and make the service conversation more useful.

Information to provide

  • Vehicle year, make, model, and trim when known
  • Whether all keys are lost or a spare exists
  • Key type: metal key, remote, fob, or push-to-start
  • Vehicle location and accessibility
  • Proof of ownership or authorized access
