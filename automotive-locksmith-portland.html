Can you make a key when every key is lost?

All-keys-lost service is available for many vehicles. The required process depends on the year, make, model, immobilizer and key system.

Can you program a key or fob I bought online?

Possibly. Compatibility must be confirmed by part number, frequency, chip type and vehicle application. Some customer-provided keys or fobs cannot be programmed.

Can you repair an ignition that will not turn?

Ignition repair or replacement may be available after the key, cylinder, steering lock and related symptoms are reviewed.

Do you unlock trunks?

Yes. Trunk unlock service is available for many vehicles after ownership or lawful authorization is confirmed.
