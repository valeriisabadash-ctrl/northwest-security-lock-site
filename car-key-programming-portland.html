Can you program a key when all keys are lost?

Many vehicles support all-keys-lost programming, but the procedure depends on the immobilizer and may require additional steps or equipment.

Why does my new key turn but not start the car?

The blade may be cut correctly while the transponder chip remains unprogrammed or incompatible.

Can programming fix a damaged fob?

Programming cannot repair broken buttons, water damage, a dead circuit board or an incompatible fob. Replacement may be required.
