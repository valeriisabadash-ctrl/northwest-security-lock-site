Modern vehicles may require cutting, chip programming, remote pairing, smart-key registration or immobilizer procedures. A key that physically fits may still fail to start the vehicle if the electronic system is not matched correctly.

Can you make a key if every key is lost?

All-keys-lost service is available for many vehicles. The exact procedure depends on the immobilizer and key system.

Can you make a cheaper spare before I lose my only key?

Often, yes. Creating a spare while a working key remains can be simpler than an all-keys-lost situation.

Can you use a key I bought online?

Only when the key, chip, frequency and part number are compatible with the vehicle.
