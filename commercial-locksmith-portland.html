Commercial security depends on the entire opening—not only the lock.

A working commercial door may involve the cylinder, key system, lever, latch, closer, panic device, hinges, frame alignment, threshold and electronic access hardware. Treating one piece in isolation can leave the real problem unresolved.

For multi-door work, provide a door count, hardware photos, access levels, employee or tenant changes and the desired outcome. A site review may be needed for larger systems or life-safety hardware.
