Commercial lock service built around control

Businesses need more than a basic lock change. Access must be organized around employees, managers, tenants, vendors, and emergency exits. Northwest Security and Lock helps Portland businesses decide whether to rekey existing hardware, replace worn components, build a master key system, or upgrade to managed entry.

For multi-door projects, prepare a door count, photos, current key setup, and a list of who needs access. That information makes the scope clearer before scheduling.

Can you create a master key system?

Yes. The correct setup depends on the number of doors, users, access levels, and current hardware. A site review may be needed.

Can existing commercial locks be rekeyed?

Many can. The locksmith will inspect the cylinders and hardware to determine whether rekeying, repair, or replacement is appropriate.

Do you service panic bars and door closers?

Commercial exit and door-control hardware can be inspected, repaired, adjusted, or replaced depending on condition and compatibility.
