What to include when you contact us

Clear details help the locksmith prepare the correct tools, hardware, and programming equipment. For an emergency, call directly. For planned service, photos can make the first conversation much more accurate.

  • Your full service address and callback number
  • The type of service: home, business, automotive, safe, or access control
  • Photos of both sides of the door and latch edge when relevant
  • Vehicle year, make, model, and key type for automotive service
  • The number of locks or doors for rekeying and commercial work

Get the right information ready for your quote

For car key replacement, share the vehicle year, make and model, whether a working key remains and whether the vehicle starts. For lock rekeying, count the doors or cylinders and explain whether you want them to use the same key. For commercial locksmith work, describe the door hardware, operating problem and any existing access-control equipment.

Ask the team to explain the proposed work, service-call charge, parts, programming and any after-hours conditions before authorizing service. Pricing and timing depend on the job, hardware compatibility and location. Be ready to establish ownership or lawful authorization for the vehicle or property. For time-sensitive lockouts, call instead of waiting for an email reply.
