What to include when you contact us

Clear details help the locksmith prepare the correct tools, hardware, and programming equipment. For an emergency, call directly. For planned service, photos can make the first conversation much more accurate.

  • Your full service address and callback number
  • The type of service: home, business, automotive, safe, or access control
  • Photos of both sides of the door and latch edge when relevant
  • Vehicle year, make, model, and key type for automotive service
  • The number of locks or doors for rekeying and commercial work
