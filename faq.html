Emergency & General Service

Are you available 24/7 for emergency locksmith service?

Yes. Emergency locksmith service is available 24/7. Call to confirm current technician availability, your location and the exact service needed.

How quickly can a locksmith arrive?

Arrival time depends on technician location, traffic, weather and the type of service. Call with your exact location for the most accurate dispatch estimate.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Portland and surrounding metro communities including Gresham, Happy Valley, Clackamas, Beaverton, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Milwaukie and nearby areas.

What information should I provide when I call?

Provide your location, callback number, service type, urgency and a clear description of the problem. For automotive work, include the vehicle year, make, model and key type.

Do I need to prove ownership or authorization?

Yes. Proof of ownership, tenancy, management authority or other lawful authorization may be required before opening, rekeying or making keys for a vehicle or property.

Can you give an exact price over the phone?

Sometimes, but not always. Accurate pricing depends on the lock, hardware, vehicle system, location, time and complexity. Clear details and photos help reduce guesswork.

Automotive Locksmith

Can you unlock my car without damaging it?

Professional vehicle-entry methods are designed to minimize the risk of damage. The correct method depends on the vehicle year, make, model and lock system.

Can you make a car key if I lost the original?

Yes, for many vehicles. All-keys-lost service depends on the year, make, model and immobilizer system. Proof of ownership or lawful authorization may be required.

Do you program transponder keys and key fobs?

Yes. Services include transponder keys, remote-head keys, key fobs, smart and proximity keys, and push-to-start programming for many vehicle systems.

Can you replace a push-to-start key?

Push-to-start and proximity-key replacement is available for many vehicles. Compatibility and programming requirements vary by manufacturer and model.

Can you remove a broken key from a lock or ignition?

Yes. Broken-key extraction is available for doors, trunks and ignitions. The lock or ignition may also need repair if it was damaged or already worn.

Do you repair or replace ignitions?

Yes. Ignition service can include diagnosis, key extraction, repair or replacement depending on the failure and vehicle system.

Residential Locksmith

Should I rekey or replace my locks?

Rekeying is usually better when the hardware works properly and you only need old keys to stop working. Damaged, worn or incompatible locks may need replacement.

When should I rekey my house?

Rekey after moving, losing keys, changing tenants, ending contractor access or whenever you are unsure who still has a working copy.

Can you install smart locks?

Yes. Smart-lock service can include compatibility checks, installation, setup and testing. Door alignment and existing hardware condition may affect installation.

Can you install high-security locks and deadbolts?

Yes. Hardware recommendations depend on the door, frame, existing preparation, security goal and budget.

Can you unlock or replace a mailbox lock?

Mailbox-lock service is available in many situations. Authorization requirements and mailbox ownership rules may apply.

Commercial & Access Control

Do you provide commercial locksmith service?

Yes. Commercial service includes rekeying, master keys, IC cores, storefront locks, panic hardware, closers, lever sets and complete door hardware service.

Can you create a master-key system?

Yes. A master-key system can provide different access levels for owners, managers, employees and maintenance staff while reducing the number of keys carried.

Do you install panic bars and exit devices?

Yes. Service includes panic bars, exit devices, related trim, closers and door adjustments. Code and life-safety requirements must be considered.

Do you install access-control systems?

Yes. Access-control work can include keypads, cards, fobs, electric strikes, magnetic locks, Trilogy locks and related commercial door hardware.

Can you rekey an office after an employee leaves?

Yes. Rekeying or changing access credentials can prevent former keys, cards or codes from continuing to work.

Do you repair commercial doors as well as locks?

Yes. Commercial door service can include alignment, hinges, closers, thresholds, sweeps and hardware replacement—not just the lock cylinder.
