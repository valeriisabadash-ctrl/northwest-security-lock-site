Explain the symptom

  • Key will not insert or turn

Do not force a key that is binding in the ignition.

A worn key, damaged cylinder, steering-lock pressure, debris, internal wafer failure or electronic problem can create similar symptoms. Forcing the key may break it or worsen the damage, so the key and ignition should be diagnosed before replacing parts blindly.

If the ignition is fine but the key itself is worn or missing, see car key replacement in Portland. If a replacement key turns but will not start the vehicle, car key programming may be the issue. For lockouts and broader vehicle lock-and-key service, see our automotive locksmith page.

Can a worn car key cause ignition problems?

Yes. A heavily worn or poorly copied key may fail to operate an otherwise serviceable cylinder.

Can you remove a key broken in the ignition?

Broken-key extraction is available depending on the break location and cylinder condition.

Will a replacement ignition need new keys?

That depends on the vehicle and replacement method. Keying and programming requirements are reviewed before the work is approved.

Why will my key go into the ignition but not turn?

The cause can include a worn key, cylinder wear, steering-lock pressure or internal ignition damage. The key and cylinder should be checked before parts are replaced.
