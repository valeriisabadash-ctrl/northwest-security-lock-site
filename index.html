Straight answers

Before you call.

These are the questions that actually help you decide what to do next.

What should I do if I am locked out right now?

Call (503) 760-1402. A direct phone call is the fastest way to explain your location, the type of lockout and how urgent it is.

Can you rekey locks without replacing them?

Often, yes. Rekeying changes which key operates the existing lock. The locksmith can tell you whether the current hardware is worth keeping.

What information do you need for a vehicle key job?

Send the vehicle year, make, model, your location, whether any working key remains and a clear photo of the key or ignition when possible.

Do you work on commercial doors and access control?

Yes. Northwest Security & Lock handles commercial lock hardware, master key needs and access-control work. Send photos and the property details for a useful first conversation.
