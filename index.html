Common Questions

Questions people ask before calling a locksmith.

Short answers, clear next steps, and no guessing games.

How fast can a locksmith arrive?

Arrival depends on current availability, location, traffic, and the service needed. Call for the current arrival estimate.

Can you rekey my locks?

Yes. Rekeying changes the lock so old keys stop working while keeping good existing hardware.

Do you replace locks or can they be rekeyed?

If the hardware is solid, rekeying may be enough. If it is damaged, loose, worn, or outdated, replacement may be better.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. Commercial service can include rekeys, door hardware, lock repair, lock installation, and security upgrades.

Can you install smart locks?

Yes. Smart lock installation depends on your door and hardware. Send photos or call so the right option can be confirmed.

Do you offer emergency locksmith service?

Emergency service may be available depending on timing, location, and current workload. If it is urgent, call directly.
