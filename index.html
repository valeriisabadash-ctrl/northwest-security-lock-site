Common questions

What customers usually need to know.

For an exact answer about your vehicle, lock, door or access system, call with the specific details.

Call (503) 760-1402
Is emergency locksmith service available 24/7?

Yes. Emergency service is available 24/7. Call to confirm the current technician availability, location and service details.

What information is needed for a replacement car key?

Have the vehicle year, make, model, location, key type and whether any working keys remain. Proof of ownership or lawful authorization may be required.

Can a car key be programmed when all keys are lost?

All-keys-lost programming is available for many vehicles. Compatibility and the required process depend on the year, make, model and immobilizer system.

Can existing home or office locks be rekeyed?

Many compatible locks can be rekeyed so the old keys stop working. Worn, damaged or incompatible hardware may need repair or replacement.

Do you install access-control systems?

Commercial services include keypads, proximity cards, fobs, electric strikes, magnetic locks, Trilogy programming and related door hardware.

Do you repair commercial doors as well as locks?

Yes. Commercial door service includes alignment, adjustment, continuous hinges, sweeps, thresholds, closers and hardware replacement.
