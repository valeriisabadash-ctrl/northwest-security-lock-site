Common Questions

How fast can a locksmith arrive?

Arrival depends on current availability, location, traffic, and the service needed.

Do you replace locks or can they be rekeyed?

If the hardware is solid, rekeying may be enough. Damaged or outdated hardware may need replacement.

Can you install smart locks?

Yes. The right smart lock depends on your door and hardware.

Can you rekey my locks?

Yes. Rekeying changes the lock cylinder so the new key set is used.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. Commercial service includes rekeys, hardware, repairs, and security upgrades.

Do you offer emergency locksmith service?

If it is urgent, call directly for current availability.
