Common Locksmith Questions

How fast can a locksmith arrive?

Arrival times depend on the location, traffic, technician availability, and service type. Emergency calls are prioritized, but call for a current estimate rather than relying on a guaranteed time.

Should locks be rekeyed or replaced?

Rekeying may be appropriate when the hardware is compatible and in good condition but old keys should stop working. Damaged, worn, outdated, or incompatible locks may need repair or replacement.

Can you install smart locks?

Yes. Compatibility depends on the door, measurements, alignment, existing hardware, and the specific smart-lock model. Photos help confirm the right next step.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. Commercial services include rekeying, master key systems, storefront hardware, panic bars, door closers, keypad locks, and access-control planning.
