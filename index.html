Better intake

Every call should start with useful details.

Bad locksmith websites create confused calls. This one tells the customer exactly what to prepare before they call or email.

  • Vehicle jobs: year, make, model, key type, and whether a working key exists.
  • Residential jobs: photos of the lock, door, deadbolt, mailbox, gate, or patio hardware.
  • Commercial jobs: business name, door photos, hardware photos, and what is broken.
  • Access control jobs: number of doors, current hardware, and who needs entry access.
  • Service area jobs: location, timing, urgency, and whether it is shop or mobile service.
