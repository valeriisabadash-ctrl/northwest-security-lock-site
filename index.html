Common questions

Answers before you call.

Locksmith work depends on the lock, property and situation. Call with the details for the most accurate answer.

How fast can a locksmith arrive?

Response time depends on your location, traffic and the current service schedule. Call with your exact area and urgency so we can give you the most accurate estimate.

Do you replace locks, or can they be rekeyed?

Many locks can be rekeyed without replacing the full hardware. We will inspect the condition and explain whether rekeying, repair or replacement makes the most sense.

Can you install smart locks?

Yes. We can help with smart locks, keypad locks and keyless entry systems, including professional installation and setup.

Can you rekey my locks?

Yes. Rekeying is a common solution after moving, changing tenants, losing keys or wanting old keys to stop working.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. Commercial services include lock repair and installation, master key systems, access upgrades and other business security solutions.

Do you offer emergency locksmith service?

Yes. Call (503) 760-1402 for emergency lockout help and tell us your location, what you are locked out of and whether there are any urgent safety concerns.
