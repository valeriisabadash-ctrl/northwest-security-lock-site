FAQ

Questions people ask before calling a locksmith.

Short answers. No fluff. No pricing promises the business has not approved.

How fast can a locksmith arrive?

Arrival depends on current availability, your location, traffic, and the type of service needed. Call for current availability and arrival estimate.

Can you rekey my locks?

Yes. Rekeying changes the lock so old keys no longer work while keeping the existing hardware when it is in good condition.

Do I need to replace my locks or can they be rekeyed?

If the lock hardware is still secure and working properly, rekeying may be enough. If the lock is damaged, worn, loose, or outdated, replacement may be the better option.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. Commercial locksmith service can include business rekeys, door hardware, lock repair, lock installation, storefront locks, tenant turnover support, and security upgrades.

Can you install smart locks?

Yes. Smart lock installation depends on the door, hardware, and lock type. Send photos or call so the shop can confirm the right approach.

Do you offer emergency locksmith service?

Emergency service may be available depending on timing, location, and current workload. If it is urgent, call directly instead of using the form.

Can I get a quote first?

Yes. Pricing depends on the lock, location, time, and situation. The shop can give a quick estimate after a few details.

Do you service cars and car keys?

Automotive locksmith help may be available. Call with the vehicle year, make, model, key type, and whether you still have a working key.

What areas do you serve?

Northwest Security & Lock serves Portland, Oregon and surrounding areas. Confirm your location when you call or request service.
