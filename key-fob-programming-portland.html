Can you program a fob I bought online?

Possibly, but only when the part number, frequency, chip and vehicle application are compatible.

Can a dead fob battery cause programming problems?

A weak or dead battery can prevent normal operation, but battery replacement will not repair internal damage or incompatibility.

Can you make a fob when every key is lost?

All-keys-lost smart-key service is available for many vehicles, depending on the immobilizer system and required procedure.
