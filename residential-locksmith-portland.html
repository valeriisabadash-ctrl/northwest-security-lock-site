Home security that fits the actual problem

Not every lock issue requires full replacement. If the hardware is in good condition but old keys should stop working, rekeying may be the smarter option. If the lock is worn, damaged, poorly aligned, or no longer secure, replacement may make more sense.

Northwest Security and Lock explains the options before work begins so homeowners, landlords, and property managers can choose the right level of repair or upgrade.

Should I rekey or replace my locks?

Rekeying is often a good choice when the hardware works well but old keys should no longer open it. Replacement is better when the hardware is damaged, worn, outdated, or not secure enough.

Can you install a smart lock I already purchased?

Many customer-provided smart locks can be installed if they fit the door and are compatible with the existing setup. Call with the brand, model, and photos.

Do renters need permission?

Renters may need property-owner or management authorization before locks are changed. Confirm permission before scheduling permanent hardware changes.
