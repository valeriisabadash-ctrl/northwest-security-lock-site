Repair, rekey or replace based on the actual hardware.

Not every lock problem requires replacement. If the hardware is compatible and in good condition but old keys should stop working, rekeying may be the better choice. Worn, damaged, poorly aligned or outdated locks may need repair or replacement.

For planned work, send the number of doors, lock types and clear photos. For a lockout, call with the address, door type and proof of authorized access.

Should I rekey or replace my locks?

Rekeying is often appropriate when compatible hardware works well but old keys should stop working. Replacement is better when the hardware is worn, damaged, incompatible or not secure enough.

Can you install a smart lock I already purchased?

Many customer-provided smart locks can be installed when the model fits the door and existing preparation. Send the brand, model and door photos before scheduling.

Do renters need permission to change locks?

Renters may need property-owner or management authorization before permanent hardware or keying changes are made.

Do you open and repair safes?

Safe opening and safe-lock replacement are available depending on the safe type, lock, condition and proof of ownership or authorization.
