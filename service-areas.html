How dispatch and travel affect service

Mobile availability changes with traffic, technician location, the type of work and whether special hardware or programming equipment is needed. A lockout, a ten-door rekey and an all-keys-lost vehicle job require very different preparation.

For planned commercial work, smart locks, multi-door rekeys or specialty vehicle keys, scheduling ahead gives the team time to confirm compatibility and bring the correct equipment.
