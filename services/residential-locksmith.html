Common questions

Should I rekey or replace my locks?

If the hardware is still good, rekeying is usually enough. If the lock is damaged or weak, replacement may be better.

Can all my locks use one key?

Often yes, if the locks are compatible. The locksmith can confirm after seeing the hardware.

Do I need service at the house or can I bring the lock in?

Some lock cylinders can be brought to the shop, but call first so they can tell you the right move.

Need home lock help?

Call with the lock issue, address area, and whether this is urgent.
