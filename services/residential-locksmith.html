Should I rekey or replace my locks?

If the hardware is still solid, rekeying may be enough. If the lock is damaged, cheap, or unreliable, replacement is smarter.

Can you rekey after a tenant moves out?

Yes. Tenant turnover and eviction rekeys should be positioned as urgent security jobs for landlords and property managers.

Can you install smart locks?

Yes, but the door and lock prep matter. Some installs need adjustment so the bolt throws smoothly.
